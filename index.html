About Us

Luv A Lollie is a little family-run sweet shop in the heart of Sheffield, Tasmania — the famous Town of Murals, nestled beneath Mt Roland and just down the road from Cradle Mountain. We hand-pick every gobstopper, twist every swirl, and jar up the brightest candy this side of the rainbow.

We’re a favourite pit-stop for travellers and tourists exploring north-west Tasmania — whether you’re heading up to the Cradle Mountain–Lake St Clair National Park, hiking Mt Roland, or wandering Sheffield’s open-air mural gallery. Pop in for a sugar rush of colour, or have our retro sweets delivered straight to your door.
