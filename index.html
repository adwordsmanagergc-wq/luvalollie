About Us

Luv A Lollie is a little family-run sweet shop tucked away in Sheffield, Tasmania — spreading peace, love and lollipops since forever. We hand-pick every gobstopper, twist every swirl, and jar up the brightest, grooviest candy this side of the rainbow.

Step inside and it’s 1972 all over again: tie-dye on the walls, sherbet in the air, and a sugar rush of colour in every corner. Whether you’re after a nostalgic retro mix bag or a giant swirl pop, we deliver the joy straight to your door.
